Our story

Beauty with a personal touch

Honey Beauty Parlour started with one simple belief: every woman deserves to feel special, not just on her wedding day but every day. Under owner and senior makeup artist Jaishree Bajaj, the studio has grown into one of Kota's most trusted names for bridal and party makeup.

Clients keep coming back for the same reasons: looks that suit them, a calm and friendly studio, and a team that treats every bride like family.
